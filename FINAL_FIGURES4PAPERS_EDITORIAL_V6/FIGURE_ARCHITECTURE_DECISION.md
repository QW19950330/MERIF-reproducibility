## Cross-figure judgment

The architecture no longer relies on repeated heatmaps. Cleveland/forest displays carry central and integrated effects; paired dumbbells carry calibration and information availability; a single heatmap pair carries calendar robustness; exact trajectories carry temporal behavior; conditional bars/points carry negative-price behavior; and ordered marker guides carry tail escalation. Market color and marker identity remain stable throughout. No market receives a privileged panel.
